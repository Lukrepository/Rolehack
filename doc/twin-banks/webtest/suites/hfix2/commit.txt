Web port: a turn at rest keeps the newest message whole on the band

With no --More-- up, a turn of the phone laid the message band out again
and showed the band's worth that holds the page's newest row (b32e17e).
Twin banks' band is narrower in one orientation than the other (260 px in
landscape against portrait's 332 on a 360x640 phone), so after the turn
the newest message often wraps onto a row more, and that band's worth can
begin between its rows: the band showed only the message's tail, the rest
of it empty, with no +N, MORE lamp or --More-- to say what had gone.  A
kill and its corpse on the 360x640 phone turned to landscape showed
"jackal corpse."; a question showed "[ynq] (n)" alone, and a tap on the
band does nothing during a question, so only another turn brought it back
(the re-check, 2026-10-03).

- web.js keepScroll(): with no --More-- up, the band laid out again shows
  the page's last rows, as many as it holds, so the newest message is
  whole whenever it fits the band.  At a --More-- the band still finds its
  place again at the start of its band's worth.  The render's mark notes
  that the band stands at the page's last rows.
- web.js moreRoom(), moreToEnd(): those last rows need not begin a band's
  worth, and only a band's worth's last row keeps room for --More--.  When
  more comes on that page before the player acts (after a text window, in
  an occupation's turns) and the band's last row runs under the --More--,
  the band first goes back to the start of its band's worth, and
  moreToEnd pages on from there: rows may show again, none is skipped.
  When the row leaves the room free, the --More-- waits where the band is.
  The same after the Layout setting is switched to classic at rest;
  classic's own turns keep its rows, and its --More--, as they were.
