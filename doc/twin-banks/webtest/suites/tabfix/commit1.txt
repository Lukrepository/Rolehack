Web port: twin banks' tablet tier, its message log and inventory panels

Twin banks now tell a phone from a tablet as the design's size classes
do, and the glass the level does not need becomes the message log and the
inventory.  Desktop mode is deferred (Lucas, 2026-10-03): every window is
laid out as for touch, so a mouse or keyboard player gets the tier the
window's size gives, with phone-size banks in the bottom corners.  The
desk (layout.js section 10, the dock and its prefix) stays in the rule
and its tests, unused by the page.

A monitor's window is a tablet now, and the tablet's 24 dp cap left most
of it black: the level at 24 dp in 2560x1440 left 52% of the window void,
3440x1440 62%.  Where a 24 dp level would leave strips wider than a panel
beside it, the cap now rises with the width.  A first cut switched to
48 dp at once.  The reviews (2026-10-03) found that a window dragged
across 2408 dp wide then went from 24 to 30 dp and moved the log, at
every pixel either side.  They also found that a monitor turned to
portrait kept its landscape cell and lost columns (1440x2560: 45 of 80
where 24 dp shows 60).  Both are fixed here.

The reviews' one major issue is fixed too.  The log decided whether to
follow its newest line from where it happened to be scrolled.  A
rotation moves the log between the glass and the tray, which reset it
to its oldest line, and a new width re-wrapped it short of its end.
After either, new messages no longer came into view.

- viewer.js: the size classes, phone under 600 dp wide or 480 tall,
  changing only 24 dp past a boundary (sizeClass, withClasses,
  classesOf).  Two tiers are kept, the window's and the device cell's,
  and only a usable layout's.
- overlay.js: rebuildTwin lays out as for touch whatever the pointer
  (pointerKind is gone), and keeps the tiers it drew for the next layout
  (twinClasses); data-tier on the root.  The geometry hands web.js the
  panels the layout placed.  guardClicks holds a touch begun on a pane
  out of the glass (#panes) to it, as it does the glass.
- layout.js:
  - deviceCell takes the cell's tier with its own hysteresis
    (prevCellTier).
  - tabletCap: 24 dp up to where the strips beside the level would be
    240 dp; over the next 160 dp of width it moves to the cell that
    fills the width, then follows it up to 48 dp (tabletWideCellMax).
    Continuous: dragged 2 px, a window's cell moves at most half a dp.
    2560x1440 shows the level at 31.5 dp, 3440x1440 at 42.5.
  - layoutCore: a portrait window keeps the cell under a level as wide
    as its own glass, never under 24 dp.  This binds only past 24 dp,
    which no phone or tablet reaches, so their two orientations keep
    one cell.
  - better(): two glasses that both show the whole level at the same
    cell go to the one above the banks (5120x2160: 14% void, not 52%).
  - fillGlass(): on 32:9 the log and the inventory stand beside the
    level.
  - Comments say desktop mode is deferred.
- web.js: the panels (layoutPanels, renderLog, renderInventory,
  panelTaps).
  - The log is the history, newest last.  A phone's log under the map
    shows only what comes before the band's first message.
  - The inventory is the core's permanent inventory, taken without a
    window (takePermInventory).  start() asks for it only for a game
    begun in twin banks, with perminv_mode:full so it lists the gold as
    INVENTORY does; classic keeps !perm_invent.  permInvent is known
    before the first layout, so a fresh page's panel no longer says the
    game began in classic.
  - A tap opens the history or the inventory; at --More-- it is Space.
  - The log follows its end, and a panel scrolled back keeps its top
    line, by the player's scrolling alone (notePlace, putPlace).  Its
    place is put back when a layout moves or resizes it.
  - A layout that moves no panel leaves its content alone.  The full
    refill at every rebuild made a dragged window's rebuild about 50%
    slower.
  - A panel is moved into the glass only when it is not already there.
    Two panels beside the level otherwise swapped places, and lost
    their scroll, at every rebuild.
  - Where the inventory has room for two columns of an item's line (a
    monitor's tray), it fills columns.  A vertical wheel moves along
    them, and the right edge fades while more lie beyond.
- rolehack.css: the panels: in the glass under its tube, or a pane of
  their own over the case; the inventory's columns; the fade.
- defaults.nh: the comment says what NETHACKOPTIONS adds for each
  layout.
- test/viewer.test.mjs: the size classes and their bands, a window
  dragged across each boundary and jittered about it, fallbacks.
- test/layout.test.mjs: 13 monitor windows (cell, whole level, panels
  clear of keys and map, void), portrait monitors, and a window dragged
  2 dp at a time wider and taller never moving its cell by more than
  half a dp (the first cut's step fails it).
- test/README.md: what the new tests check.
