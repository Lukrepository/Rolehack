Web port: a question's chips stand clear of the ANSWER pill

A reviewer playing at 443x939 (2026-10-03) met "This door is locked.
Kick it? [ynq] (q)" with the hero in the map's top-left corner.  The
chips go to the map's foot when the hero is near its top, and the ANSWER
pill, which twin banks show at the map's bottom-left while a question's
answers are on the pad, was drawn over the 'y' chip and hid its label.
The pad's Yes still answered, but the chip meant for the same answer was
unreadable.  The overlap is older than the tablet stage: placeChips()
never knew of the pill, and it placed the chips before the pad's answers
put the pill up.

- web.js: placeChips() puts the chips' foot over the layer's pill while
  a layer is up (chipBottom()), and shim_yn_function places them again
  once the answers are on the pad.  With no pill up, or the chips at the
  map's top, nothing changes.

Checked in the browser (Playwright, Chromium, density 1 and 2.4375, no
console errors): webtest/tabfix/dcheck.mjs asks the reviewer's question
through the page's own yn handler, with the hero at (2, 2) so that the
chips go to the map's foot, at 443x939, 1024x768 and 896x443.  On
10b91db the pill lies on the 'y' and 'n' chips at all three windows.
Here no chip touches it, every chip stays inside the map, and 'q'
answers.  The header stage's chips suite (placement, scrolling,
taps) and the layers stage's suites, whose pop-up checks include the
pill and the chips, pass on this tree, and so do the rest of the suites
listed in 10b91db.
