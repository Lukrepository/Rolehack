Web port: a slow swipe of REST no longer counts as the old Pray hold

The re-check of the layers stage (2026-10-03) found one more way to set
off the ghost deck's Pray-habit guard.  In landscape REST sits where
classic's SACRIFICE was.  REST's slot has a second gesture: the swipe
up that shows Long rest (Lucas, 2026-10-02) and the swipe down that
puts it away.  The strip follows the finger, so a deliberate swipe easily
lasts 380 ms from touch to lift.  Any such swipe flashed 'SACRIFICE ->',
swallowed the next step and marked the ghost deck's session caught,
which keeps the deck from retiring.  The last fix let only REST's count
hold off, because only the count layer marked a hold as used.

A hold that did something with its time is no habit.  Each touch on the
old spot now carries its own mark, holdMeant(), and each gesture marks
it for itself:
- the count layer, when a count is picked or the layer is left up;
- REST's strip, once it slides;
- a key's own hold that fired where the key reaches into the spot's
  8 dp: M1's macro editor, a hub's layer.  Before this, M1's editor at
  844x390 came up under a SACRIFICE flash, and the y after it was eaten.

The mark used to be one slot for the whole page.  Now it belongs to the
hold, so the other thumb's hold meanwhile marks nothing.  GAME's and
WORLD's lift is their tap, so a 380 ms press of them sets nothing.  GAME
is the whole spot in portrait and at 640x360, and up to a third of it on
a phone in landscape.  As designed, the arrow there still shows over the
drawer the tap opened, and the drawer's backdrop takes the y.

- overlay.js: prayHabit() keeps a record per touch, { id, at, meant }.
  holdMeant(id) marks it, and prayLift() keeps it until the key's own
  lift has run.  holdUsed is gone.  bindScrollWell() marks the touch
  once the strip drags.  countHold()'s release marks it as before.
  bindHold() marks it when its hold fires, unless a held layer's
  release decides.  The comment over prayHabit() says what counts.
