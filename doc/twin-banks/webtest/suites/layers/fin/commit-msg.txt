Web port: twin banks' layers on the pad, and the near-miss guard

Twin banks paint every layer on the movement pad and guard the banks
against near misses (the design's sections 6 to 8, v2).  A thumb that
misses a key by a few dp must neither walk the hero nor fire another
key, and nothing pops up over a key: the count rows, the pad centre's
radial and CONTEXT's candidates, which the last stage opened inside the
map, become places on the pad, the same thumb motion in either
orientation.  Lucas's decisions of 2026-10-02 stand: Long rest swiped
up out of REST, an empty pin's tap opens its picker, Pray on the 380 ms
hold of SACRIFICE.  Classic is unchanged.

The stage's tester and two reviewers (2026-10-03) found, and this fixes:
- Chrome's touch adjustment gave a thumb's touch to a key from the case,
  the glass or a band just past a halo: 14 dp above REST rested 20
  turns, at --More-- a tap on the status band beside SACRIFICE offered,
  and in portrait a tap 14 dp past the left bank armed the right bank's
  COMBAT.  A touch Chrome moves onto a bank now goes back to what lies
  under the finger, and between two keys the guard's own geometry
  decides, to the fraction of a px.
- The 120 and 200 ms rules were timed at the map touch's lift, so a
  double tap on a drawer item travelled.  They are timed from where the
  tap landed, and a prompt's chips count as a closing.
- From CONTEXT the stairs' second tap was the lit up or down arrow, the
  very tap that aims after a near miss of COMBAT, and it changed level.
  The second tap is CONTEXT's or the pad centre's, which no aim uses.
- Every REST count hold in landscape set off the old Pray habit's guard:
  it flashed SACRIFICE, ate the next step and kept the ghost deck from
  retiring.  Only a hold that set nothing is the habit now.
- Smaller: a tap queued while the core travels went unguarded; HERE's
  ALL was out of the pad centre's reach; the lit count was amber on
  amber (1.12:1); twin's PIN 2 Fire leaked into classic's pins; a turn
  lost the stairs lit in CONTEXT's HERE; a hub layer's 4 s ran from the
  hold, not the lift; 1 px off a keycap at 360 dp went to its neighbour.

- overlay.js, the guard: each bank key's element is its hit cell, the
  keycap drawn inset (Key.cell, guardGeometry); the cells tile each bank
  to the screen's edges and the pads own the gap above them.  Under the
  cells a 12 dp halo snaps a touch within 8 dp to its key and swallows
  the rest with a tick and a 150 ms flash; the action pad's seams
  swallow; halos and seams stand over the scrim for the keys lifted over
  it.  retarget() undoes Chrome's touch adjustment across the guard's
  ground and off the banks, and bankAt() resolves a touch between keys
  by the cells, not by Chrome's px square.  keyDistance() and RING_REACH
  give web.js its ring; rolehackGuardLog() lists what the guard did.
- overlay.js, the layers: COUNT from REST, SEARCH, a counted CONTEXT and
  Long rest (↖ ×1, ↑ ×5, ↗ ×10, → ×20, the centre typed; ×100 to ×400),
  picked by a slide, a chord or a tap, closing on the lift unless held
  600 ms, the count in use lit on a dark cap.  HERE from the pad centre,
  slide-only, ALL by sliding back onto the centre; from CONTEXT it stays
  up.  Its places are fixed, dimmed when the turn does not offer them.
  The stairs from CONTEXT light the pad centre for 2 s, and CONTEXT or
  the centre goes.  A layer drops back to arrows after 4 s idle, counted
  from any touch or key, its landing and its lift, or on a map tap; a
  turn keeps the layer, its stickiness and the stairs lit in it.  The
  layer's name is on the pill inside the map.
- overlay.js, the rest: the FLICK legend's nodes wear their strokes
  (↑ Kick, ↗ the second macro) and take a tap or a hold while FLICK is
  held or a command is being placed; the wedges split at -62.5 degrees.
  A drawer item held to pin it keeps its touch (spendTouch), so the pin
  is in hand.  Twin keeps its attack pins under their own name, PIN 2
  starting on Fire (twinPins).  The ghost deck guards the two old
  habits: a pad-centre hold lifted without a slide, and a hold of the
  old SACRIFICE spot that set nothing.
- web.js guardTap(): while a map tap would travel (the core waits for a
  command, or is busy and the tap would queue for the next one), a map
  tap disarms an armed Fight or closes a layer and goes no further;
  every tap within 200 ms of a drawer, window, menu or chip row closing
  is swallowed; a tap in the confirm ring previews its cell, a second
  tap there within 2 s walking, and a ring tap within 120 ms of a key's
  lift is swallowed.  At --More--, in getpos and under a menu or a form
  the guard steps aside.
- commands.js: the HERE group, HERE's ALL drawer.
- feedback.js: tick(), the swallow's and a dimmed place's light buzz.
- prefs.js: atkSlotsTwin, twin's own attack pins.
- rolehack.css: the keycap inset in its cell, dimmed places, the halos'
  and seams' flash; the twin pop-up rows go.
