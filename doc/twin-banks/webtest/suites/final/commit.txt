Web port: the log scrolled back keeps its top line, full history or turned

The tablet stage promised that the message log, scrolled back, keeps the
line at its top through new messages and turns of the device (10b91db).
The final integration pass (2026-10-04) ran its re-check's own probes
again (webtest/tabrecheck drift.mjs and log.mjs) and found two ways in
which it did not:
- Once the history is full (HISTORY_MAX, 256 messages, which a game soon
  reaches), each new message drops the oldest line from the log's top.
  The panel was redrawn at the same scrollTop, so the view moved one line
  on with every message: five looks moved a log scrolled back by five
  lines.
- A turn re-wraps the top line itself.  Its offset into the line was put
  back in px, so a line scrolled 22 px into its three rows in the tablet's
  tray (62 px) went back 21 px into its one row in the portrait glass,
  wholly above the top, and the next line came to the top at every turn.
Only twin banks have panels; classic is untouched.
- web.js:
  - renderLog() counts the lines gone from the log's top since it was last
    drawn (the panel's first), and fillPanel() keeps the top line by its
    place in the new list; when that line is gone itself, the oldest line
    left.
  - notePlace() notes the top line's height with its offset, and
    putPlace() and fillPanel() put back the same share of it, short of its
    end (lineOff()).
Checked in the browser (Playwright, Chromium, real CDP touches and turns,
density 1 and 2.4375, no console errors):
- tabrecheck/drift.mjs: a full history scrolled back, then 5 messages:
  the same line at the top (before: 5 lines on).  With 103 lines: kept, as
  before.
- tabrecheck/log.mjs: all pass at both densities (before: 2 failures at
  each, the full history's 3 messages and its turn).
- Every suite that fills, moves or scrolls a panel, again at both
  densities:
  - tabrecheck inv and chips; tabfix fixes and dcheck;
  - tablet panels, tablet and resize;
  - the tester's screens 306/306, panels 68/68, rotate, wheel, monitor
    and classic 56/56;
  - the tablet reviewers' probes z2, z3, z4, z9, z10 and z11, whose output
    is the same as before the change.
- The final pass's own checks (webtest/final):
  - every spec.json screen in a new game, 464/464 at each density;
  - two minutes of play on Lucas's phone turned mid-fight, 24/24;
  - turns with something up, 15/15;
  - classic, 12/12.
- node --test win/web/test/: 147/147.
