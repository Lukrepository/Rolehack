Web port: a key's tap no longer clicks what it opens under the finger

A key acts on its pointerup, and the browser's click for the same touch
comes after it, on whatever is under the finger by then.  When the key
has just opened a window there, the click presses that window's control.
Testing the twin banks layout (the review, 2026-10-02) found it twice:
INVENTORY sits where a long inventory's ESC lands, so the list closed as
it opened, and MENU's tap landed on the Settings form's Case row and
turned the case back on for a caseless player.  Classic's keys happen to
have no window control under them, but the click was sent all the same:
a control put over INVENTORY on its pointerup is clicked by that touch.
A click that ends a touch begun on a key can only press something the
finger never went down on, so it goes in every layout.

- overlay.js: guardClicks(), from the constructor.  Capture-phase
  listeners note a touch that goes down on #keys and when it lifts; its
  click (the pointer the click names, else the last such touch to lift)
  is dropped when it lands outside #keys within a second.  A click inside
  the keys, from a touch begun anywhere else, or from the keyboard (no
  pointer), passes as before.

Checked in the browser (Playwright, Chromium, real CDP touches, the
same restored game; webtest/fix3/ghostclick.mjs): on HEAD's page with
only this change, at 896x443 and 443x939, a button put over the whole
screen on INVENTORY's pointerup is not clicked by that touch, while a
touch begun on the button still clicks it once; MENU then DONE leaves
the case off.  The same check on HEAD itself clicks the button, as the
review found.  No console errors.
