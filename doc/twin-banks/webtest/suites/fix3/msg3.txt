Web port: the twin banks on the page, the default layout; classic kept

Lucas, 2026-10-02: a setting "Layout: twin banks / classic", twin by
default, and classic working as it always has.  Twin banks are the
design's guarded twin banks (layout.js): each thumb owns one 3x6 bank in
its own bottom corner, every size and offset from the device's short and
long sides, never the orientation, so both orientations get the same
banks; the movement pad keeps the player's key size and is never scaled,
and the map takes what the banks leave.  His decisions stand as he gave
them: Long rest is swiped up out of REST, an empty pin's tap opens its
picker, the map may still be dragged along an axis where the level fits
(locked and centred only at rest), and Pray stays on the 380 ms hold of
SACRIFICE.  A window with no room for twin banks shows classic, and the
setting stays twin.

A tester and two reviewers checked the first cut, and what they found is
fixed here:
- the remembered budget: a first visit in a window that is not the whole
  device turned (Lucas's phone beside a wiki in split screen, 443x460)
  estimated a landscape height of 1 dp and drew keys of 0.17 dp instead
  of classic; an iPhone's Safari, whose portrait toolbar landscape does
  not have, got a 35 dp pad; and every temporary window (a split half, a
  short landscape window) was remembered, so the next full window got 46
  dp keys.  viewer.js now decides which windows use and teach the budget;
- the ghost deck counted sessions played in classic or in portrait, where
  it can catch nothing, and retired before a player ever met it;
- a pinch in twin wrote classic's zoom, and a classic zoom overrode
  twin's map cell: twin now has its own zoomFactor (the design's
  section 11);
- twin's paranoid_confirmation line came after the player's own option
  lines and overrode them;
- at --More--, a tap on the status lines did nothing; a second tap on
  COMBAT re-armed Fight instead of disarming it, and the pad did not tint;
  PIN 1 and PIN 2 vanished while the COMBAT layer was open; hold hints
  were clipped on 44.7 dp keys ("AP TO FIL").
The tester's other suggestion, a budget cut-off inside layout.js (h under
0.6 w), is not taken: the page no longer passes a guess that does worse
than no budget, and a real landscape height that costs portrait a step
of key size is the parity the rule is for.

- viewer.js (new): budgetFor() and budgetedLayout().  Only a portrait
  window across the screen's short side, or a landscape one across 85%
  of its long side, uses the budget or teaches it; any other window lays
  out alone.  The landscape height is estimated from the screen only when
  the portrait bars it takes are a browser's (150 dp at most), and any
  estimate is kept only when its layout is no worse than the window's own
  (no smaller pad, not degraded).  It is learnt only from a usable,
  undegraded layout, and the landscape height only grows.
- overlay.js: rebuild() keeps classic as rebuildClassic(), unchanged, and
  adds rebuildTwin(): the size from the svh root, the display mode, the
  safe-area probe, touch or mouse, the text sizes and the budget, then
  layout().  Every key is placed at its layout rect in CSS px with no
  scale; the case paints the two wells; the lamps move onto the keys
  (SEARCH, ARMED on COMBAT or the pin that armed, MORE at the message
  band's end).  The count rows, the pad centre's list, the CONTEXT
  candidates, the flick legend, the banner, the layer pill and the key
  flash open inside the map; the drawers are 3 columns, bottom-anchored
  in it.  A rebuild keeps an armed Fight, open layers, count rows,
  drawers and an assignment, and waits for a finger to lift or a text
  field to let go.  The ghost deck: in landscape a map tap on an old deck
  key's spot previews, and a second tap on the same cell walks; it counts
  only turns played where it could act, and retires after three such
  sessions with nothing caught.  In twin a second tap on COMBAT disarms
  Fight and the pad tints red while armed; the pins stay, dimmed, under
  the COMBAT layer; a hold hint too wide for its key gives way to its
  last word, then shrinks.  Settings: "Layout" (with the reason when this
  window shows classic or squeezes the banks) and "Old key spots"; Reset
  zoom resets twin's factor in twin.
- web.js: layoutTwinGlass(): the glass, the stacked bands and a canvas
  exactly the map area, on device pixels.  The map tap path holds the
  ghost deck's preview.  tileSize() is the device cell times zoomFactor
  in twin, and the pinch writes the factor on its lift; classic's zoom
  code is as it was.  At --More--, a tap on twin's glass outside the map
  and the band is Space.  start() adds paranoid_confirmation:+Remove for
  twin only, before the player's own lines.
- prefs.js: layout (twin), budgets ({ w, h, l, sl, sr, pw, lh } per
  display mode), ghostDeck, zoomFactor (1).
- rolehack.css: twin only: the svh root and the inset probe, the wells,
  the MORE lamp, the ARMED lamp and the armed pad's tint, the layer pill,
  the ghost flash, Long rest's upright strip, the windows lifted over the
  system keyboard.
- defaults.nh: a comment and a commented copy of the twin line; classic's
  options are unchanged.
- build.sh, sw.js: viewer.js is copied and cached.
- test/viewer.test.mjs (new), test/README.md: the budget against the
  rule: first visits in splits, touch PCs, iPhones and three-button bars
  lay out as the window alone (443x460 unusable); Lucas's tab keeps its
  first visit's banks after the turn; no temporary window changes a full
  window's keys, cell or budget; a desktop window teaches nothing.
